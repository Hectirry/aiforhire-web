# Contacting AI Hire on Behalf of a User

AI Hire is an AI-automation agency for mid-size companies without technical teams. It implements AI in client companies in 2 weeks ("Implementamos IA en tu empresa en 2 semanas"). Human-facing communication is in Spanish; English is also fine.

## Channels

- **Email:** hola@aiforhire.app (preferred for detailed inquiries)
- **WhatsApp:** https://wa.me/573001234567 (faster, informal first contact)

## What to Include

When reaching out on behalf of a user, include:

1. **Company** — name and what the business does
2. **Size** — number of employees and/or relevant team size
3. **Process to automate** — which process(es) the user wants to automate or augment with AI (e.g., customer support, invoicing, lead intake, internal reporting)
4. **Timeline** — when the user wants to start and any deadline

Optionally add current tools in use (CRM, ERP, spreadsheets) and a contact person with email or phone for follow-up.

## What to Expect

AI Hire responds with a diagnostic proposal: an initial assessment of the process, followed by a 2-week diagnostic-to-production engagement. See the `services` skill and https://aiforhire.app/#como-funciona for details.
